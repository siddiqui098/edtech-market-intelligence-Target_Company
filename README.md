# EdTech Market Intelligence

**An End-to-End Data Analytics Project | Python | MySQL | SQL | Power BI**

## Project Overview

EdTech Market Intelligence is an end-to-end data analytics project examining the financial performance, enterprise-learning performance, educational reach, learner engagement, and monetization trends of five selected EdTech organizations using publicly available data.

The project combines Python-based data acquisition and processing, MySQL database management, SQL analysis, and Power BI dashboards to investigate real-world business questions.

## Organizations Covered

| Organization | Analytical Focus |
|---|---|
| Coursera | Financial performance and learner reach |
| Udemy | Financial performance and enterprise learning |
| Skillsoft | Financial performance and enterprise learning |
| Duolingo | Financial performance, engagement, and monetization |
| Khan Academy | Educational reach and learner engagement |

Khan Academy is analyzed separately because its nonprofit model differs from the commercial EdTech organizations.

## Key Business Questions

**1. Commercial Financial Performance**
- How has revenue evolved across Coursera, Udemy, Skillsoft, and Duolingo?
- How has profitability changed over time?
- Which companies demonstrate sustained revenue growth?

**2. Enterprise Learning**
- How have enterprise customer metrics evolved for Udemy Business and Skillsoft?
- How have Udemy Business ARR and Net Dollar Retention changed?

**3. Duolingo Engagement and Monetization**
- How have DAU, MAU, and the DAU/MAU ratio evolved?
- How have paid subscribers and subscription bookings changed?

**4. Khan Academy Educational Reach**
- How has registered-user reach evolved?
- What do reported learning minutes and active-learner metrics indicate?

## Tools and Technologies

- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and visualization
- **GitHub:** Version control and project documentation

## Project Scope

- Data collection covers 2021–2026, subject to public data availability.
- 2026 observations are treated as year-to-date (YTD), not full-year results.
- The main financial and engagement dashboard comparisons cover FY2021–FY2025.
- Khan Academy academic-year reporting is preserved separately.
- Company-specific metric definitions and reporting limitations are considered during analysis.

## Analytical Considerations

Publicly reported metrics are not always directly comparable. Differences in business models, fiscal years, metric definitions, and reporting scope are documented.

Missing or unreported observations are represented as `NULL`, not zero. Comparisons and conclusions are limited to what the available evidence supports.
